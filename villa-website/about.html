ABOUT OUR VILLA

Experience a Complete Lifestyle

Our Story & Vision

Welcome to Jayasooriya GEM VILLA, a sanctuary of luxury and comfort located just 500m from the vibrant Boralasgamuwa Town. We pride ourselves on offering an exceptional living experience that goes beyond standard accommodation.

Our villa is designed to be your home away from home, featuring a variety of room types—including Double Rooms, Deluxe Double Rooms, and our expansive 181m² Deluxe Villa. Each space is meticulously crafted with modern aesthetics and essential amenities like high-speed WiFi and full air conditioning.

Whether you're lounging by our 24/7 private swimming pool, preparing a feast in our gourmet kitchen, or enjoying the serenity of our landscaped surroundings, Jayasooriya GEM VILLA is committed to making your stay unforgettable. We invite you to experience the perfect blend of luxury, convenience, and Sri Lankan hospitality.
